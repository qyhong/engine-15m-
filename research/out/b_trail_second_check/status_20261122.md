## 4. Where the registered rule is not precise

1. **When exactly it is read.** Line 203 ("the first server scoreboard at or past that count") and line 368 ("no earlier
   than 10 Dec 2026") are not joined in one sentence. Not stated: the time zone and hour on 10 Dec, and that all trades
   to that moment count (about 130, not 60).
2. **The 60 and 100 checks collide.** At the current pace the book passes 100 trades about 3 Dec, before the second check
   may be read. Read literally, the "last check at 100" would come first. The row "ahead at 60 but not at 30 -> same test
   at 100" then has no meaning.
3. **What "ahead of the fresh main copy in a market" means.** The rule says `vs_main_usd` for the total. For the
   per-market count it names no figure. `vs_main_usd` is marked equity (open trades valued at the last close) from
   `page.json` `shadows.scoreboard`, while the "ahead" verdict is from `scoreboard.json` (closed trades only). Not
   stated: which file and timestamp give the per-market figures, how a tie or a missing market counts, or how markets
   that are closed at that moment (the S&P 500 out of cash hours) are marked.
4. **The consequences no longer fit.** The pass outcome is already done on six markets (v5.2.8). The rule says nothing
   about what a result against the hold copy means for those six.
5. **The yardstick is right but fragile** (section 3a): the hold side is a handful of open trades.
