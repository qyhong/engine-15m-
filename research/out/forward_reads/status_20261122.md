## The 30th `b_targets` trade

- The 29 are counted the way the scoreboard counts them: one row per trade, and 29 rows give 29 trades (no merges).
  `shadows.run` keeps a Target 1 half in `part` and writes the trade once, at its final exit, with the tags joined
  (`T1+T2`, `T1+STOP_BE`; `engine15/shadows.py` lines 225 and 256). So a half closed at Target 1 never shows up as a
  row on its own. `per_entry` is applied per market file, so BTC and ETH entries at the same minute (both 11 Nov 06:45)
  are not merged.
- The 30th trade is whichever of the eight open `b_targets` positions fully closes first: Brent short (since 18 Nov),
  BTC short and ETH short (since 21 Nov 14:30), S&P short, USDJPY long, WTI short (Target 1 already taken; it counts
  only when the rest closes), silver short and gold short. It may be a non-crypto market, so it need not be one that
  members follow.
- In the first scoreboard that shows 30 or more, the line will read: `trades` 30 (31 if two trades close in the same
  step), net, mean, 90% range, won %, and one of `ahead: even the low end of the range is positive` /
  `behind: even the high end of the range is negative` / `undecided: the range includes zero`. All eight markets are
  pooled and the comparison is with zero.
